# Representations

## What it is
Representations are the numeric vectors or features that describe text.

## The key intuition
A good representation preserves meaning: similar texts should have similar vectors.

## Why it matters
This is the bridge between raw text and any machine learning model. If the representation is weak, the model cannot learn the right patterns.

## Common forms
- Bag-of-words or TF-IDF
- Embeddings and sentence vectors
- Similarity scores such as cosine similarity or Levenshtein distance
