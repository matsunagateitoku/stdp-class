# Modeling

## What it is
Modeling is where you teach a machine to make predictions from text representations.

## The key intuition
A model is just a function that learns patterns from examples. The better the examples and representations, the better the model.

## Why it matters
This is the step where you move from understanding text to doing something useful with it: classification, clustering, summarization, or named entity extraction.

## Common modeling tasks in this repo
- Classification: `Train a Classification Model To Categorize Text.ipynb`
- Clustering and similarity: `class_5/Practice_Embedding.ipynb`, `Practice_Comparing_Sequences.ipynb`
- Topic modeling and summarization: `Identifying Topics From Documents.ipynb`, `Extracting Summary Sentences From Documents.ipynb`
- Named entity recognition and sentiment: `class_6/NER.ipynb`, `class_6/Conduct_Sentiment.ipynb`

## Remember this
Think of modeling as the “what can I do with this text?” stage. The answer depends on the question you are asking.
